How You Can Help

Together we can support students in need.

Donate Food

  • What to donate: Non-perishable food only — canned food, pasta, rice, maize meal, cooking oil, long-life milk, hygiene products and sanitary pads.
  • Drop-off location: Vanderbijlpark Campus Building A14, Mahikeng Campus Building E7, Potchefstroom Campus Building E7A
  • Drop-off times: Monday to Friday, 08:00 - 16:00

Volunteer

  • What you will do: Pack food parcels, help with Friday distribution, support wellness campaigns, peer mentoring
  • Time commitment: 2-4 hours per week. Flexible hours
  • Who can volunteer: All NWU students and staff
  • How to sign up: Go to Building A14, Split Level 2

Financial Donations

  • What it funds: Meal vouchers, textbooks, and hygiene packs for students in need
  • How to donate: EFT to NWU Student Support Fund. Banking details provided upon request
  • Transparency: Monthly donation reports available upon request
